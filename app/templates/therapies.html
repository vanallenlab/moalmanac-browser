Therapies

Therapies are the individual cancer treatments referenced by propositions in the database. Each therapy has a therapy type defined by the National Cancer Institute: chemotherapy, hormone therapy, immunotherapy, or targeted therapy. The proposition and statement counts include those where the therapy is given as part of a combination.
